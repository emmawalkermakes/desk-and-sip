Desk & Sip is an independent hobby blog about the small, everyday objects that make a space feel like yours: a tumbler with your name on it, a phone case with a photo you love, a desk that doesn't look like everyone else's.

It's written in Brisbane, often from a small desk in a warm room, which means a soft spot for things that keep drinks cold, styling that works in a rental, and decor that copes with humidity and strong sun.

## What you'll find here

- **Personalisation ideas** for tumblers, cups, phone cases and desk accessories.
- **Small-space styling** for apartments, share houses and rentals.
- **Practical notes** on care, materials and what actually lasts.
- A free [photo size checker](/photo-size-checker/) to see if your phone photo is sharp enough to print at a given size.

No sponsored posts, no fake reviews, and no made-up statistics.

## Disclosure

This is a hobby blog. I enjoy making things, and now and then I may feature something I make myself. When I do, I'll say so clearly in the post.
